The prototype

  1. 1

    Click Build

    On the coffee story map, pick Lovable and the MVP slice.

  2. 2

    Copy the prompt

    One prompt holds the whole plan.

  3. 3

    Paste it into Lovable

    Paste, send, and leave it to build.

  4. 4

    Watch it build

    It reads the story map, builds the app and says what it made.

Click through it

  1. Pick a drink and a size from the menu

  2. The order adds up, ready to pay

  3. Pay, and the order is confirmed

  4. The coffee shop sees the order and marks it ready

Check it yourself. The prototype is live at coffee-shop-mvp.lovable.app

The full app

The full app comes from the same coffee story map, with every slice in the prompt and not just the MVP. Lovable added photos of the drinks, milks and syrups, pickup or delivery, and a shop dashboard with the takings for the day. Payments are simulated.

Check it yourself. The full app is live at coffee-app1.lovable.app

Note. During the build, we asked Lovable's AI how useful the story map was as a spec. It answered: "The storymaps.io YAML spec was exceptionally well-suited for guiding the build. Compared to typical PRD documents or free-form tickets, it provided a very clean balance of structure and clarity."
