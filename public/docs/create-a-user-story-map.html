Create a user story map

3 min read

On this page

No sign-up, no trial, nothing to install. Click Start Mapping on the home page and you have a story map with its own link, ready to edit.

  1. 1Start Mapping opens a new, blank story map.
  2. 2Take the Tour walks you through story mapping with a coffee app example, and shows the app as it goes.
  3. 3The Jira, Asana, Phabricator and Linear buttons import a backlog and lay it out as a story map.
  4. 4Try a sample story map opens your own copy of a finished example.

What a new story map has

A new story map starts with one user, one activity, one step and one task, ready to rename, all in one slice. Click any card to type in it, and click Untitled map at the top to name the story map.

Build it in this order

Build a story map from the top down and from left to right. Get the journey right first, then fill in the work under it, then cut it into releases.

  1. 1The backbone. Name the users, the activities they're trying to do, and the steps of their journey. Define the story map backbone
  2. 2The tasks. Add the work under each step, most important at the top. Add tasks to a story map
  3. 3The detail. Open the tasks that need it and write the story behind them. Write user stories on a story map
  4. 4The releases. Slice the story map into releases, starting with a thin first slice across the whole journey. Plan releases with story map slices

Read the steps out loud from left to right before you go into detail on any one of them. If one step doesn't lead to the next, there's a step missing, and it's cheaper to find now than after the tasks are in.

Tip. Building it with the team? Run a user story mapping session follows the same order.

What works on every card

  • Type. Click a card to type in it. A new card has starter text, such as New Step, which is selected when you click, so typing replaces it.
  • Change it. Hover over a card and click its … for its colour, status, points, tags and link. Customise story map cards
  • Move it. Drag a card to a new place. Drag a step by the ↔ at its top left, and everything under it moves too.
  • Add around it. Right-click a column to add a step before or after it, add a spacer column, duplicate the step or delete the column.

Click ↩ at the top to undo, and ↪ to redo. On the keyboard, press Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y to redo, or Cmd on a Mac.

The link is the story map

Each story map has its own link, such as storymaps.io/abc12345, and every change saves as you make it. To get back to it:

  • Bookmark it. When you name a story map, the app reminds you once to bookmark it.
  • Open a recent map on the home page lists the story maps you've opened in this browser.

Tip. Anyone with the link can edit the story map. If you're sharing it publicly, it's wise to lock it from Menu → Lock Map (Read-only) and create a backup from Menu → Backups, in case of unexpected changes.

From inside a story map

The main menu has the same ways to start:

  • New starts another blank story map.
  • Copy this map makes a copy at a new link, and leaves the original as it is.
  • Samples opens an example as a new story map, after asking first.
  • Take a Tour starts the tour.
