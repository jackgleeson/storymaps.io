Find your story maps

2 min read

On this page

Every user story map has its own link, such as storymaps.io/abc12345, and the link is how you get back to it.

Bookmark it

When you name a story map, the app shows a one-time reminder to bookmark it, just below the name.

  • Copy link copies the link, to keep somewhere safe or send to the team.
  • Got it closes the reminder.
  • Don't show again stops the reminder on every story map in this browser.

Press Ctrl+D, or Cmd+D on a Mac, to bookmark the page. On a phone, add it from the browser menu.

Open a recent story map

The app keeps a list of the story maps you've opened in this browser, newest first, with when you last opened each one.

  • On the home page, click Open a recent map under Start Mapping.
  • Inside a story map, open the main menu and choose Recent maps. The story map that's open shows as Open now.

The browser Back button closes the list.

A story map you opened inside a project shows as the project, with its number of story maps, and opens on the story map you last had open there.

Note. The list lives in this browser only, and is never sent anywhere. Another browser or device has its own list, so bookmark the story maps you want to keep.

Tidy the list

  • Click × on a row to take it off the list.
  • Click Clear list to empty it.

Neither deletes a story map. It still opens from its link. A story map that no longer exists leaves the list when you try to open it.

Share the link

Anyone with the link can open the story map, so the link is also how the team finds it. Copy it from Share → Share URL, and put it wherever the team talks about the work. Share a story map has more.

Tip. Anyone with the link can edit the story map. If you're sharing it publicly, it's wise to lock it and create a backup, in case of unexpected changes.
