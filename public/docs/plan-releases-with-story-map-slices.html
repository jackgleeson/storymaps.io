Plan releases with story map slices

3 min read

On this page

Slices group the cards you'll build together, such as an MVP and the releases after it. A slice runs across the whole user story map, so you can see what each release covers from start to finish.

Try it now

Plan the releases of a small coffee app. This opens your own copy of the story map, so change anything you like.

Open practice story map

Tip. Make the first slice thin: only the minimum implementation each step needs to work and be useful, so users can get from the first step to the last in the simplest way that works. A first release that does all of one step, and none of the rest, can't be used. Slice an MVP with a story map shows the difference.

The slice menu

Everything you can do to a slice is in its menu.

  1. 1Click the ☰ in the strip on the left of the slice, or right-click anywhere in the strip.
  2. 2Copy link to slice copies a link that opens the story map at this slice.
  3. 3Mark Complete folds a finished release into a banner.

Add a slice

Click + Slice in the strip to add a new slice straight under that one. Drag a card from one slice to another to move it to a different release.

Give a slice a goal and a date

Click a slice's name to open its details. Here you can rename the slice and give it a goal and a target date.

The goal and date show behind the ⓘ next to the name, so they're there when you need them without taking up room on the story map. On a phone the details open at the top of the screen, clear of the keyboard.

Tip. Keep a goal short enough to read at a glance, such as "Order and pay ahead, then pick up".

Link to a slice

Every slice has its own link. Opening it scrolls the story map to the slice and highlights it for a moment, which is handy in a chat message or a ticket.

Renaming the slice never breaks the link, and you can copy it from a locked story map too. Inside a project, the link keeps the project, so the tabs are there when it opens.

Finish a release

When a release ships, choose Mark Complete from the slice menu. The slice folds into a banner that shows its goal and how many cards it holds, and its cards stay safe inside. If some cards have a status other than Done, you can move them to the top of the next slice first, or mark the slice complete anyway.

Click the banner to see the cards without reopening the slice. They show read-only, and only for you. Choose Reopen to unfold the slice for everyone and edit its cards again.

Close folds a slice the same way without marking it complete, for a release you've set aside.

Reorder slices

Drag a slice by the ↕ handle in its strip to move it up or down the story map.

Track progress

The strip of each slice shows how many of its tasks are done, as a bar and a percentage, and the total story points of its cards. For every slice at once, open the Overview tab.

Note. Progress counts the Yellow cards, and any other card with a status. A card in another colour with no status, such as a Cyan note or a Lime question, doesn't count.
