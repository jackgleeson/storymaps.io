Reduce duplication with partials

2 min read

On this page
Make a partial Use it again See what's inside Change a partial Delete a partial

Journeys often share steps. Signing in, paying or checking out can turn up in more than one place, with the same cards under them each time. A partial holds those steps once. Put it wherever the journey needs it, and change it in one place.

Make a partial

  1. 1Click the background of a step, away from its name, to select it.
  2. 2Shift-click another step to select every step in between.
  3. 3Click Create Map Partial in the toolbar that appears, and name the partial.

The steps move into the partial, with every card under them in every slice, and a single partial step takes their place. It shows the name of the partial, how many steps it holds, and its first and last step. The Partials tab opens with the new partial in it.

Note. Selecting steps needs a mouse, so partials are made on a computer. Once made, they show on every device.

Use it again

  • Right-click a column and choose Add partial step after, then pick the partial.
  • To swap steps you've already written out for a partial, select them and click the ▾ beside Create Map Partial. Choose Replace with and the name of the partial. The button changes to match, so click it to replace the steps.

Note. Replace with swaps the selected steps and their cards for the partial. Ctrl+Z undoes it.

See what's inside

Under a partial step, each slice says how many of its cards are inside, such as Show 3 cards. Click it to open the steps of the partial in place, with their cards. Click Hide cards above the steps to fold them back into one.

In the Partials tab, Expand and Collapse do the same for one partial, and Expand All for every one. Opening a partial changes the view for you only, not for anyone else on the story map.

Change a partial

In the Partials tab, click Edit on the partial. Its steps open on the story map, ready to change like any other: rename them, add cards, or add a step in the space at the end. Rename the partial in the tab too. Click Done when you're finished.

Every place the partial is used shows the change.

Delete a partial

Click Delete in the Partials tab, then choose one:

  • Delete Partial removes the partial, and every partial step that uses it.
  • Restore Columns puts its steps back on the story map as ordinary steps, with their cards.

Deleting the last partial step on the story map removes the partial too, after asking first. Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Tip. Partials reuse steps inside one story map. To keep several whole story maps together, start a project.
