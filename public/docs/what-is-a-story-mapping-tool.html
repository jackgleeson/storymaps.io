What is a story mapping tool?

3 min read

On this page

A story mapping tool is software for building a user story map online. The team lays out the journey, adds the work under each step and slices it into releases, in one place that everyone can open and change.

What a story mapping tool does

A story map has a set shape: steps run from left to right, tasks sit under each step, and slices run across them. A story mapping tool is built around that shape:

  • Every task sits under a step and in a slice, so the columns and rows stay lined up.
  • Moving a step brings its tasks with it.
  • Several people can work on the same story map at once.
  • Each task can hold its own detail, such as a body, a status, points and tags.
  • The work can go on to the tracker the team already uses.

Sticky notes, whiteboards and story mapping software

A story map works in any of them, so pick what suits the team.

  • Sticky notes on a wall suit a team in one room. Everyone can stand at the wall, move the notes around and talk it through.
  • An online whiteboard brings people in different places together on one board.
  • Story mapping software is built around the shape of a story map. The whole team edits it live from one link, each release shows its goal, date and progress, and the cards can go to your tracker or to an AI tool to build from.

Many teams map on a wall first, then move the story map into a tool to plan each release from it.

What to look for in a user story mapping tool

  • Slices for releases, each with a goal and a date.
  • Live editing, so everyone sees changes as they happen.
  • Quick invites, so everyone in the session can join from one link, with no account to create first.
  • Progress, with a status and points on the cards.
  • A link to your tracker, so cards don't need copying across by hand.
  • Structured data behind every story map, in standard formats such as YAML, so AI tools and scripts can read it, and you can move it to another tool.
  • Backups and locks, to record progress, undo accidental changes and hold an agreed plan.

Storymaps.io

Storymaps.io is a story mapping tool that runs in the browser, with no sign-up needed, no paywall and no trials. Click Start Mapping on the home page and share the link with the team.

  • Plan releases with story map slices covers releases, goals and dates.
  • Edit a story map together covers working live.
  • Review release progress on a story map covers status and points.
  • Jira, Asana, Phabricator and Linear integrations cover sending cards to a tracker.
  • Export a story map covers JSON, YAML and CSV files.
  • Story map data links give the data behind any story map, always up to date, and the Storymaps CLI edits it as YAML from the terminal.
  • Back up and restore a story map records progress with a note at each milestone, and restores an earlier version if something is changed by accident.
  • Lock a story map keeps an agreed plan exactly as it is, until it's ready to be worked on at the next session.
  • Start a story map project covers grouping story maps as tabs.
  • Build with AI turns a slice into a working prototype, so product teams can build to learn.
