Bulk edit story map cards

2 min read

On this page

Select a group of cards and change them all in one go: moves, colour, status, tags, copies or deletes. One action, one undo.

Try it now

Select cards on a finished coffee app story map. It opens as your own copy, so change anything you like.

Open practice story map

Select cards

  • Drag a box around the cards, starting from an empty part of the story map. Every card the box touches is selected.
  • Click a card away from its text to select it on its own. Clicking the text edits it instead.
  • Shift+click another card to add it, and Shift+click a selected card to remove it from the selection.
  • Shift+click a step after selecting another step to select every column between them.

Click an empty part of the story map, press Esc or click × on the toolbar to clear the selection.

Note. Selecting works with a mouse on an unlocked story map. On a phone or tablet, change cards one at a time.

The selection toolbar

The toolbar shows at the bottom of the screen as soon as anything is selected.

  1. 1How many cards and columns are selected.
  2. 2Duplicate and Delete act on the selected cards. Click ▾ beside either to switch it to whole columns.
  3. 3Color, Status and Tags change every selected card at once.

Move cards together

Drag any selected card, and the others travel with it.

  • Cards from several columns stay in their own columns and only move up and down, so each task stays under its step. Use this to move a batch of tasks into another slice. A pale card in each column shows where each one will land.
  • Cards from one column can go anywhere, and stack together where you drop them.

Ctrl+Z undoes the whole move.

Change colour, status or tags

  • Color picks one colour for every selected card. × puts them back to their default colour.
  • Status sets Done, In Progress, Planned or Blocked, or No Status to clear it.
  • Tags lists the tags already on the selection, with × to take one off every card. Below them are the other tags in the story map to add, and a box to type a new one.

The colour and status changes show in the progress bars and the Overview tab straight away. Customise story map cards explains what each one is for.

Duplicate

Duplicate Cards copies the selected cards into new columns, straight after the selection. Switch to Duplicate Columns to copy whole columns, with their steps and every card under them. Ctrl+D (⌘D on a Mac) duplicates the selected columns too.

Delete

Delete Cards removes the selected cards, and Delete Columns removes whole columns with every card in them. Both ask first. Delete or Backspace on the keyboard deletes the selected cards, or the columns when steps are selected.

Tip. Deleted the wrong thing? Ctrl+Z undoes it. Before a big clear-out, make a backup.

Turn columns into a partial

Select two or more columns and the toolbar offers Create Map Partial. A partial holds a run of steps that more than one journey shares, such as checkout, so you write it once and use it anywhere. Reduce duplication with partials covers how partials work.
