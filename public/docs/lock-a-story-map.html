Lock a story map

1 min read

On this page
Lock it What people can do with a locked story map Unlock it Once it's unlocked In a project

A lock makes the user story map read-only for everyone without the password. Share the plan wide, and keep the edits to the people who own it.

Lock it

  1. 1Open Menu → Lock Map (Read-only).
  2. 2Type a password of at least 4 characters.
  3. 3Click Lock Map.

Everyone else on the story map goes read-only within seconds, and sees This map is read-only across the top of the screen. You can keep editing in this tab.

Tip. Keep the password somewhere safe, as it can't be recovered.

What people can do with a locked story map

Anyone with the link can still open it, search it, share it and export it. They can't change cards, slices or notes, import into it, or restore a backup.

Unlock it

Open Menu → Unlock Map, type the password and click Unlock. The story map stays unlocked for you until you close the tab. Everyone else still sees it locked.

Once it's unlocked

The menu has three more options while you have it unlocked:

  • Re-lock Map locks it again with the same password, in this tab too.
  • Change Password sets a new password. The old one stops working.
  • Remove Lock takes the lock off, so anyone with the link can edit again. The app asks first.

Locks, unlocks and removed locks all show in the Log.

Tip. Lock a story map when it matters to keep it exactly as it is, such as the release the team agreed. It's then the reference everyone works from. Unlock it when it's time to plan again or make updates, and lock it again once they're agreed.

From the terminal

storymaps lock locks the story map, and storymaps unlock saves the password on your computer so pushes still work. See Lock and unlock a story map.

In a project

A locked story map shows a padlock on its tab.

To lock every story map in a project at once, lock the project.
