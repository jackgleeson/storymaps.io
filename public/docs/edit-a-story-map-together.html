Edit a story map together

2 min read

On this page

Everyone with the link edits the same user story map, at the same time. Changes show for everyone as they happen, with no saving and no refreshing.

Try it now

Open a practice story map, then copy its link into a second window to see changes go both ways.

Open practice story map

Bring people in

Copy the link from Share → Share URL and send it to the team. There's no sign-up, so the link is all anyone needs. Share a story map covers the other ways to share.

Your name

The first time you open a story map on a computer, the app asks for a name for live collaboration. It's optional. Skip it and you show as guest.

To change it, click your name at the top right and type a new one.

Your name stays in this browser. Others on the story map see it, and it goes in the Log next to your changes.

See who's here

When someone else is on the story map, a count of everyone there shows beside your name. Hover over it to see who they are, with you first. People on a phone show as Mobile user. Several tabs in the same browser count as one person.

Follow each other

On a computer, everyone else's pointer moves across the story map with their name on it. When someone drags a card or a column, you see it move with them.

Too busy? Click the crossed-out pointer in the controls at the bottom right to hide other pointers. Click it again to bring them back.

Tip. On a call, point at the card you're talking about. Everyone sees your pointer, so "this one" is enough.

Keep notes together

Click Notes in the tabs at the bottom right for a notepad that everyone on the story map shares. Type at the same time and each person shows by name where they're typing.

Use it for decisions, actions and anything that doesn't belong to one step. The notes save with the story map and go with it when you export it.

If the connection drops

Keep working. A notice at the bottom of the screen says you're offline, and your changes sync as soon as you reconnect. The app warns you if you try to close the page before they have.

Tip. Anyone with the link can edit the story map. If you're sharing it publicly, it's wise to lock it and create a backup, in case of unexpected changes.
