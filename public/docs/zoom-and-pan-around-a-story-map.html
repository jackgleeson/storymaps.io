Zoom and pan around a story map

1 min read

On this page
Zoom Pan Fit the whole story map On a phone or tablet Find your way

A user story map opens zoomed to fit the width of the window, so you see the whole journey first. Zoom in to work on part of it, and pan to move around.

Try it now

Move around a coffee ordering story map.

Open practice story map

Zoom

  1. 1+ zooms in.
  2. 2The percentage shows the zoom. Click it to step through 100%, 75%, 50%, 25% and 10%.
  3. 3− zooms out.

Hold Ctrl and scroll, or Cmd on a Mac, to zoom in on the spot under the mouse. The story map zooms from 10% up to 300%.

Pan

  • Scroll to move up and down, and hold Shift and scroll to move sideways.
  • Hold the right mouse button and drag to move in any direction.
  • Press the arrow keys to move a little at a time.

A right-click that doesn't move still opens the menu for that column.

Fit the whole story map

Press Alt+R to zoom the story map back to fit the window. On a Mac, press Shift+0.

The story map fits itself again when the window changes size, such as when you go to full screen.

On a phone or tablet

Swipe with one finger to move around, and pinch with two to zoom. On a small screen the story map never fits below 50%, so the cards stay readable. Swipe sideways for the rest of the journey.

Tip. On a tall story map, freeze the top rows so the steps stay in view as you scroll down.

Find your way

  • Search fades every card that doesn't match, so the ones you want stand out.
  • A link to a slice opens the story map at that slice.
