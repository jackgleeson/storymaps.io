Search and filter a story map

2 min read

On this page
Search Filter Filter by a tag Add tags to cards Close the search

Search fades every card that doesn't match, so the ones you're after stand out where they sit on the user story map. Filters narrow it down by status, colour and tag.

Try it now

Search and filter a finished coffee app story map, with statuses and tags on its cards.

Open practice story map

Search

Click the magnifying glass at the top, or press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac). Type in Search cards... and the cards that don't match fade as you type.

Search reads the text on every card: users, activities, steps and tasks. It ignores capitals, so "pay" finds Pay, Apple Pay / Google Pay and Handle payment failures?.

Filter

Click the funnel beside the search box to open the filters.

  1. 1Status shows cards with any of the statuses you tick, including No Status.
  2. 2Color shows cards in any of the colours you pick.
  3. 3Tags shows cards with any of the tags you tick.

Options that no card uses are greyed out. Set more than one section and a card has to match each of them, such as In Progress and tagged backend. A search and filters work together too.

The funnel shows how many filters are on. Clear All turns them off, and Done closes the panel and keeps them.

Filter by a tag

A tag can go on any card, so a tag filter pulls together related work from different steps and slices. On the coffee app story map, tick ai under Tags and click Done. The four AI cards stay bright across V2 and V3, and the rest fade.

Tick a second tag to see the cards with either one. To narrow it down, tick something under Status as well, such as Planned.

Add tags to cards

Tags are labels you choose yourself, added to one card or to many at once. They're always saved in lower case.

One card

Hover over the card, click … and go to Tags.

  1. 1The tags on the card. Click × to take one off.
  2. 2Type a tag and press Enter, or click one of the tags offered.

Several cards

Select the cards by dragging a box around them or with Shift+click, then click Tags on the toolbar. Bulk edit story map cards has more on selecting cards.

  • The tags at the top are already on the selected cards. A number beside one shows how many have it, when it isn't all of them. Click × to take it off every selected card.
  • Click a tag in the list below to add it to every selected card.
  • To make a new tag, type it in New tag... and press Enter.

Tip. Tag cards by team or area, such as frontend and backend, and a filter shows one team the work that's theirs across every release. Customise story map cards covers tags.

Close the search

Click × beside the search box, or press Esc. The first Esc closes the filters and the next closes the search. Closing clears the search and every filter, and the story map is back to normal.

Note. Search and filters only change what you see. Everyone else on the story map sees it as usual.
