Take the story mapping tour

2 min read

On this page
Start the tour Move through it What it covers After the tour

The tour walks you through story mapping on one example: a coffee ordering app. It points at each part of the story map in turn, from the people at the top to the releases underneath.

Start the tour

There are three ways to start it. Use whichever is closest:

  • From the home page: click Take the Tour.
  • From inside a story map: open the main menu and choose Take a Tour. If the story map has anything on it, the app asks you to confirm first. The browser's back button brings you back to it.
  • From a link: go to storymaps.io/tour.

The tour opens its own copy of the coffee app story map, at a new link.

Move through it

  1. 1Skip ends the tour at any point.
  2. 2Back goes to the step before.
  3. 3Next moves on. On the last step it reads Finish.

The keyboard works too: → or Enter for the next step, ← for the one before, and Esc to end the tour. The dots under the text show how far through you are.

The tour works on a phone too. Tap Next and Back to move through it.

The tour holds the story map still while each step is showing, so the mouse wheel doesn't scroll. It runs in light mode, and puts dark mode back when it ends.

What it covers

  1. 1What a story map is for, and the coffee app example.
  2. 2The backbone: who the users are, what they're trying to do, and the steps of their journey from left to right.
  3. 3Tasks under each step, with the most important at the top.
  4. 4Release slices: the MVP, then a Later slice for everything that can wait.

On the MVP, the tour invites you to drag some cards around. Go ahead: they return to their places when you move on. The Later slice is there to show a second release, and is removed when the tour ends.

After the tour

The coffee app story map stays at its link, and it's yours. Change it, or use it as the start of a story map of your own work.

To start from a blank story map instead, see Create a user story map. What is a user story map? covers each part in more detail.
