Change the story map view

1 min read

On this page
Focus mode Full screen Dark mode Other people's cursors Frozen rows

The view controls at the bottom right change how the user story map looks to you. None of them change the story map, or how anyone else sees it.

Try it now

Try the view controls on a coffee ordering story map.

Open practice story map
  1. 1Focus mode hides the status, points, tags and links on cards, for a cleaner story map to present.
  2. 2Full screen fills the screen with the story map.
  3. 3Dark mode switches between light and dark.
  4. 4Cursors shows or hides other people's cursors.
  5. 5Freeze rows keeps the top rows in view as you scroll down.

Focus mode

Focus mode leaves just the titles on the cards. The ⤢ still shows when you hover over a card, so you can open one for its detail.

Full screen

Full screen fills your screen with the app, without the browser around it, and zooms the story map to fit. Press Esc twice to leave it, so one press can still close anything that's open, such as search or a card. Some browsers leave full screen on the first press.

Dark mode

The story map starts in the same theme as your device. The button switches it, and your browser remembers the choice. On the home page, the switch is in the main menu.

Other people's cursors

When others are on the story map, you see their cursors and names move around. Hide them when they get in the way, such as when presenting.

Frozen rows

On a tall story map, frozen rows keep the users, activities and steps at the top while you scroll down, so you can always see which step a card is under.

You can freeze the users, activities and steps, just the activities and steps, just the steps, or nothing. The choice is saved for your browser only.
